04 The method

Measure the rhythm.
Trace the lag.
Keep the evidence.

  1. 01

    Detect

    FFT, Welch, and Morlet-wavelet spectra locate recurring frequencies. Peaks are refined below the grid spacing, so a tone scores the same wherever it falls.

    ⌁
  2. 02

    Root

    Lagged correlation or transfer entropy scores every directed pair. Circular-shift surrogates keep each series' own memory and give family-wise adjusted p-values.

    ⌖
  3. 03

    Measure

    Resonance depth combines spectral concentration, persistence, phase coherence, and amplitude stability. The score is unchanged if you relabel Hz as cycles per month.

    ⟶

A directed edge is a lead–lag relationship under a chosen estimator and null model, not a proof of causation. A result in one domain does not carry over to another.
