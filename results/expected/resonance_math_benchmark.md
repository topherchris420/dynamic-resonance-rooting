# Phase and harmonic mathematics for DRR relationship tests

Generated by `python scripts/run_resonance_math_benchmark.py --output-dir results/expected`
from `drr-resonance-math-benchmark-v1` (config SHA-256 `eebc6fbaaded…`,
preregistration SHA-256 `d7e5fd7a0396…`, matches the frozen
preregistration: yes). Every value is a Monte Carlo estimate on simulated channel
pairs with a known truth. The JSON beside this file stores one character per trial, and
every rate below is recomputed from those characters.

Shared settings: alpha = 0.05. DRR rooting searches lags 1–4 with 99 circular-shift surrogates and max-statistic correction. The phase test searches ratios up to order 3 within 0.08 of the observed frequency ratio, filters with a Gaussian band of standard deviation f0 × 0.1667, and calls a non-significant pair inconclusive below 20 effective phase samples. Missing values and irregular timestamps are interpolated linearly onto the regular grid before any method runs.

Bold marks an inflated rate on an unrelated condition: the Wilson 95% lower bound of the rate of declared relationships exceeds 0.05.

## Methods

| Method | Family | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `drr_rooting` | existing DRR | lagged correlation, lags 1-4, 99 circular-shift surrogates, max-statistic correction |
| `drr_rooting_te` | existing DRR | the same test with the transfer-entropy backend (pyinform) |
| `pearson_analytic` | conventional | Pearson correlation at lag 0, Fisher z test assuming independent samples |
| `coherence_analytic` | conventional | Welch magnitude-squared coherence at the first channel's peak, threshold (1 - C)^(K - 1) for K segments |
| `coherence_shift` | conventional statistic, shift null | the same coherence against every circular shift of the second channel |
| `plv_rayleigh` | conventional | 1:1 phase-locking value with the Rayleigh threshold exp(-N PLV^2) assuming independent samples |
| `phase_sync` | proposed | n:m phase-locking value, Farey ratio search, exact seam-corrected shift test |
| `phase_sync_1to1` | ablation | phase_sync with the ratio fixed at 1:1 |
| `phase_sync_no_seam` | ablation | phase_sync without the seam-correcting phase ramp |
| `phase_sync_no_trim` | ablation | phase_sync without trimming filter edges |
| `phase_sync_broadband` | ablation | phase_sync on the Hilbert phase of the unfiltered series |
| `oriented_area` | geometry | normalized oriented area against every circular shift, two-sided |
| `drr_plus_phase` | proposed, combined | related if drr_rooting or phase_sync is significant at alpha / 2 (Bonferroni) |
| `phase_sync_gated` | proposed, gated | phase_sync, abstaining when the data-quality gate abstains |
| `drr_plus_phase_gated` | proposed, combined, gated | drr_plus_phase, abstaining when the data-quality gate abstains |

## Preregistered hypotheses

A method is controlled on an unrelated condition when the Wilson 95% lower bound of its rate of declared relationships is at most alpha, and inflated when that bound exceeds alpha. A detection rate is the rate of declared relationships on a related condition. Abstentions are never counted as declared relationships. Hypotheses are evaluated on held-out conditions only. The rule, conditions, seeds, trial counts, methods, thresholds, and hypotheses were fixed before the held-out conditions were first run and are not changed after the rows are seen.

| Id | Statement | Rule | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | The phase-synchrony test is controlled on every stationary unrelated held-out condition, including the phase-randomized negative control and independent oscillators at identical or harmonically related frequencies. | phase_sync is controlled on every stationary unrelated condition | **supported** |
| H2 | Each conventional baseline with a textbook threshold (Pearson correlation, Welch coherence, Rayleigh phase-locking value) is inflated on at least one stationary unrelated held-out condition. | each of pearson_analytic, coherence_analytic, plv_rayleigh is inflated on at least one stationary unrelated condition | **supported** |
| H3 | On every held-out harmonic coupling (1:2 and 2:3), the phase-synchrony test detects the relationship at least 0.25 more often than existing DRR rooting. | phase_sync detection rate minus drr_rooting detection rate >= 0.25 on every harmonic condition | **supported** |
| H4 | The ratio search carries H3: with the ratio fixed at 1:1 the phase test detects each held-out harmonic coupling in at most 15% of trials. | phase_sync_1to1 detection rate <= 0.15 on every harmonic condition | **supported** |
| H5 | The seam correction is load-bearing: without it the phase test is inflated on the held-out coherent identical-frequency condition, where the corrected test is controlled. | phase_sync_no_seam is inflated and phase_sync controlled on the coherent condition | **supported** |
| H6 | Existing DRR rooting is inflated on the held-out coherent identical-frequency condition. | drr_rooting is inflated on the coherent condition | **supported** |
| H7 | Without the gate, the phase-synchrony test is inflated on the held-out shared-sweep condition, because its stationarity assumption fails there. | phase_sync is inflated on common_chirp | **supported** |
| H8 | The data-quality gate abstains in at least 80% of trials on the held-out random walks and shared sweep, and in at most 10% of trials on every stationary held-out condition. | gate abstention >= 0.8 on random_walk and common_chirp and <= 0.1 on every stationary condition | **supported** |
| H9 | On the stationary held-out 1:1 couplings, the phase-synchrony test's mean detection rate is no more than 0.10 below existing DRR rooting's. | mean phase_sync detection rate on the stationary 1:1 couplings is at least the mean drr_rooting rate minus 0.10 | **not_supported** |
