## Preregistered hypotheses

A null is controlled on a process when the Wilson 95% lower bound of its family-wise error is at most alpha. It is inflated when that lower bound exceeds alpha. The rule, the processes, the seeds, and the trial counts are fixed before the run and are not changed after the rows are seen.

| Id | Statement | Rule | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | The circular-shift null is controlled on every stationary process, including fractional Gaussian noise at H = 0.95 and GARCH(1,1) noise. | every circular_shift row on a stationary process is controlled | **supported** |
| H2 | The phase-randomization null is controlled on every stationary Gaussian process, which is fractional Gaussian noise at every H. | every phase_randomization row on a stationary Gaussian process is controlled | **supported** |
| H3 | The permutation null is inflated on every long-memory process, which is fractional Gaussian noise with H > 0.5. | every permutation row on fractional Gaussian noise with H > 0.5 is inflated | **supported** |
| H4 | The circular-shift null is inflated on the random walk, because its stationarity assumption fails there. | the circular_shift row on the random walk is inflated | **supported** |
| H5 | The phase-randomization null is inflated on the random walk, because its stationarity assumption fails there. | the phase_randomization row on the random walk is inflated | **supported** |
| H6 | Long memory costs power, not size: with the circular-shift null, the lag-2 edge at coupling 0.3 is recovered less often at the largest H than at H = 0.5. | circular_shift true-edge rate at the largest H is below the rate at H = 0.5 | **supported** |
