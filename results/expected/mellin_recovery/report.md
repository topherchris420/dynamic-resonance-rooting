# DRR-Mellin hidden-mode recovery benchmark

Generated by `scripts/run_mellin_recovery_benchmark.py`; do not edit by hand. Schema `drr-mellin-recovery-benchmark-v1`, preregistration SHA-256 `a6fbf4ed6de44c77…` (matches the frozen record: yes). Every rate is a Monte Carlo estimate on simulated records, recomputed from the per-trial codes in the JSON artifact.

A trial is **recovered** when |omega_hat - omega| <= pi / ln(x_max / x_min) (half a Rayleigh width in log-frequency). **Detected** means the method's statistic exceeds the threshold calibrated on noise-only records of the same design and noise model (nominal false-alarm rate 0.05).

## Methods

- `drr_mellin_scan` (DRR-Mellin (primary)): drr_framework.mellin.log_mode_profile on the raw samples: joint least-squares scan over 17 growth exponents and 581 log-frequencies; statistic 1 - relative SSE.
- `drr_log_spectrum` (DRR-Mellin (secondary)): drr_framework.mellin.log_spectrum with its documented beta = 0.5 weighting on a uniform ln-x grid (linear interpolation when samples are not log-uniform); statistic peak / mean power in the search band.
- `lomb_scargle` (established): scipy.signal.lombscargle of the mean-removed samples against ln x on the same frequency grid; statistic 2 x peak power / total sum of squares.
- `nls` (established): scipy.optimize.least_squares fit of exp(beta u)(a cos omega u + b sin omega u), u = centred ln x, started at the Lomb-Scargle peak with beta = 0; statistic 1 - relative SSE.
- `esprit` (established): least-squares ESPRIT, model order 2, Hankel depth n // 3, on a uniform ln-x grid (linear interpolation when samples are not log-uniform); statistic energy fraction of the two leading singular values.
